Mercury is a dependency-free Ruby statevector simulator (up to 12 qubits in-browser). The CPSC panel mirrors src/cpsc/analyzer.py: gates are admitted only if they preserve the Z2 parity sector — a compile-time rejection rule, not post-selection. Trotter preset evolves the transverse-field Ising H under test.
